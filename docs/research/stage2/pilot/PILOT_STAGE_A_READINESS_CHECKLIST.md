# Pilot Stage A Readiness Checklist

**Status:** Not ready for annotation. This draft is a preparation aid; no cases were selected or reviewed.

## Protocol and case design

- [ ] Owner reviews the annotation, selection, and reviewer drafts.
- [ ] Approved 10-resolved / 5-unresolved workflow scope is preserved; no performance objective, pass criterion, accuracy claim, or coverage floor is added.
- [ ] Open decisions are resolved/dispositioned, especially evidence sufficiency, unresolved search boundary, and uncertain-control replacement.
- [ ] Sampling frame, inclusion/exclusion rules, identity grouping, and leakage checks are set before prediction/candidate access.
- [ ] Prior V3/Day 5 eligibility is decided without inspecting predictions.

## Evidence and provenance

- [ ] Proposed resolved cases meet the independent person-level plus publication-level evidence standard, or are deferred from the resolved group.
- [ ] Sources are independent of HAMMER and the candidate source; URLs/IDs, access dates, evidence dates/periods, and locators are recordable.
- [ ] Contradictions, inaccessible sources, and failed searches have recording/escalation paths.
- [ ] Unresolved controls use an approved search boundary; no nonexistence is inferred from an empty provider result.
- [ ] Source terms, privacy, access rights, retention, and secure storage are reviewed; restricted captures stay out of Git.
- [ ] `gold_person_id` is distinct from provider IDs; storage is reviewed without modifying benchmark templates.

## Reviewers and blinding

- [ ] Two distinct Stage A reviewers can independently review every case.
- [ ] Separate adjudicator and disagreement process are available.
- [ ] Conflicts of interest and reviewer-ID handling are settled.
- [ ] Packets contain no candidates, ranks, scores, resolver states, explanations, or historical labels.
- [ ] Reviewers cannot access each other's judgments before submission or Stage B materials before Stage A freeze.
- [ ] Confidence anchors and time-measurement method are defined or explicitly omitted by owner decision.

## Storage and freeze

- [ ] Case ledger, evidence references, reviews, adjudication, and workload log have approved paths/access controls.
- [ ] File format/provenance fields are agreed; this CSV remains empty until authorized.
- [ ] Stage A label and identity-group freeze is defined before candidate capture.
- [ ] Reporting uses exact counts, denominators, and suitable descriptive intervals; no pass/fail threshold is added.
- [ ] Owner separately authorizes annotation after readiness review. Candidate generation, Stage B, and pilot execution remain gated.
